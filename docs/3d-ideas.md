# 50 ideas for new 3D components

Ideas that suit the dimensions-ui style: procedural geometry, one or two
physical interactions, every colour exposed as a prop. Grouped by theme.

## Everyday objects with a satisfying moving part

1. Mechanical keyboard with per-key press and switch sound
2. Zippo-style lighter: flip the lid, strike the wheel, flame flickers
3. Pocket knife with blades that fold out one by one
4. Retractable tape measure: pull the blade, it snaps back
5. Padlock: spin the dial, shackle pops when the code is right
6. Stapler that bends open to reload
7. Fountain pen with a cap that posts on the barrel
8. Analog alarm clock with ringing hammer bells
9. Desk lamp with an articulated arm you can pose
10. Watch box with a lid and cushion insert

## Kitchen and drink

18. Kettle with steam and a whistle that rises in pitch
