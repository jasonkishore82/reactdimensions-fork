# 50 background ideas

Backdrops for the 3D scenes and the landing page, grouped by the feel they
give. Most are cheap: a shader plane, a few instanced meshes, or an
Environment tweak.

## Studio and product-shot backdrops

1. Infinite curved cyclorama (sweep) in a single tinted colour
2. Gradient sweep that shifts hue to match the product's main prop colour
3. Soft horizon line: floor and wall meet with a blurred band
4. Spotlight pool: one bright disc on the floor, falling off to dark
5. Rim-light backdrop: dark wall with a thin bright edge behind the object
6. Paper roll backdrop with a visible curl at the bottom
7. Dark velvet with subtle sheen for metal and glass
8. Frosted acrylic plate under the object with a glow from below

## Geometric and drafting

9. Fine drafting grid that fades out with distance
10. Isometric grid floor with the object's footprint highlighted
11. Dimension lines on the backdrop that measure the model
12. Blueprint: dark blue wall with white hairline grid and corner marks
13. Graph-paper floor that tilts toward the camera
14. Dotted matrix floor where dots brighten under the model's shadow
15. Concentric rings on the floor centred on the model
16. Chevron floor stripes that scroll slowly to suggest motion

## Light and atmosphere

17. Volumetric light shafts through slatted blinds
18. Caustic ripples on the floor, as if under shallow water
19. Slow-moving soft bokeh discs behind the object
20. Dust motes drifting in a light beam
21. Fog gradient that swallows the floor edges
22. Sunrise sky dome with a warm band at the horizon
23. Overcast sky with moving cloud shadows on the floor
24. Neon strip lights reflecting in a wet floor

## Architectural and spatial

25. Gallery wall with a floating shelf and a wall-wash light
26. Concrete alcove with a soft skylight
27. Wooden workbench surface with scratches and a pegboard behind
28. Marble slab with veining matched to the accent colour
29. Window light: a bright rectangle with mullion shadows
30. Minimal plinth on a reflective floor
31. Shoji screen with backlit paper panels
32. Tiled bathroom wall for the bottle and perfume ideas

## Motion and generative

33. Slow particle field that parts around the model
34. Flowing noise ribbons in the accent colour
35. Reaction-diffusion pattern evolving on the back wall
36. Audio-reactive bars for the turntable and speaker scenes
37. Falling leaves or petals with ground collision
38. Rain streaks on a glass pane between camera and model
39. Snow accumulation on the floor plane
40. Terrain heightfield that gently breathes

## A single default

To replace all current flat backgrounds with one consistent look, the tinted
cyclorama with an accent-matched gradient and a faint fading grid gives the
most consistency across the library for the least work.
