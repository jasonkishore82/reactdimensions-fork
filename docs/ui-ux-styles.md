## 1. Flat UI

Solid colours, no shadows or textures, simple icons and clear type.
Traits: flat fills, bold colour blocks, crisp edges.

### 1.1 Colour-block dashboard
- **Look:** White canvas split into solid colour panels, one bright hue per data area; no gradients or shadows.
- **Buttons:** Solid rectangles with 6px corners, white label on the brand colour; hover darkens the fill by one step.
- **Nav pill:** Full-width top bar in the brand colour; the active tab is a white pill with brand-coloured text.
- **Sidebar:** Solid dark-blue column with white line icons; the active item gets a flat lighter-blue block behind it.
- **Use case:** Analytics dashboards and internal admin tools.

### 1.2 Two-tone onboarding
- **Look:** Each screen uses two flat colours, a background and one accent, with a large flat illustration.
- **Buttons:** Wide pill buttons in the accent colour with bold labels; secondary actions are plain text links.
- **Nav pill:** Progress dots under the illustration; the current step stretches into a short pill.
- **Sidebar:** None during onboarding; a skip link sits in the top corner.
- **Use case:** Mobile app sign-up and first-run walkthroughs.

### 1.3 Icon-first utility app
- **Look:** Grid of flat, single-colour icons on a light grey background with short labels underneath.
- **Buttons:** Square icon tiles that fill with colour when pressed; text buttons only for confirm or cancel.
- **Nav pill:** Bottom tab bar with flat icons; the active tab gets a coloured pill behind its icon.
- **Sidebar:** Collapsible list of flat category chips, each with its own colour dot.
- **Use case:** Calculators, converters, file managers and other tool apps.

### 1.4 Flat e-commerce catalogue
- **Look:** Product photos on flat pastel squares, price in bold, generous white gutters.
- **Buttons:** Solid "Add to bag" bar in black; quantity steppers are flat outlined squares.
- **Nav pill:** Horizontal scroll of category pills; the selected one is filled black, the rest outlined.
- **Sidebar:** Filter panel of flat checkboxes and colour swatches in plain sections, with no dividers beyond thin lines.
- **Use case:** Fashion and homeware online shops.

### 1.5 Flat docs site
- **Look:** Plain white pages, one accent colour for links and code highlights, simple sans-serif type.
- **Buttons:** Small flat buttons for "Copy" and "Edit on GitHub", outlined in the accent colour.
- **Nav pill:** Segmented control to switch between language or framework versions of the code samples.
- **Sidebar:** Left column of section titles; the active page gets an accent-coloured bar on its left edge.
- **Use case:** Developer documentation and help centres.

## 2. Glassmorphism

Frosted, semi-transparent panels floating over a colourful background.
Traits: background blur, light translucent fill, thin bright border.

### 2.1 Frosted music player
- **Look:** Album art blurred to fill the screen; controls sit on a frosted glass card in the middle.
- **Buttons:** Round glass buttons with a white 1px border; play/pause is larger and slightly more opaque.
- **Nav pill:** Floating glass pill at the bottom with Library, Search and Now Playing; the active tab is a brighter glass chip.
- **Sidebar:** Glass queue panel that slides in from the right, showing the next tracks over the blurred art.
- **Use case:** Music and podcast players.

### 2.2 Weather glass cards
- **Look:** A sky gradient that changes with the weather, with stacked frosted cards for hourly and daily forecasts.
- **Buttons:** Small glass chips for units (°C/°F) and location; tapping adds a soft inner glow.
- **Nav pill:** Glass pill at the top with Today, Week and Radar.
- **Sidebar:** Saved-locations list on a tall glass panel, each city showing a tiny live temperature.
- **Use case:** Weather apps and smart-home wall displays.

### 2.3 Glass login screen
- **Look:** Vivid gradient blobs behind one frosted login card with a light border and soft shadow.
- **Buttons:** Primary button in solid white with dark text for contrast; social sign-in buttons are glass.
- **Nav pill:** Glass toggle at the top of the card for Sign in and Sign up.
- **Sidebar:** None; a small glass help bubble sits in the corner.
- **Use case:** Login and sign-up pages for SaaS products.

### 2.4 Glass finance overview
- **Look:** Deep blue-purple background with glowing orbs; balance and card details on frosted panels.
- **Buttons:** Glass action tiles for Send, Request and Top up, each with a coloured icon.
- **Nav pill:** Floating glass pill for Overview, Cards and Activity, with a sliding highlight.
- **Sidebar:** Frosted left rail with icon-only navigation that expands to labels on hover.
- **Use case:** Banking and crypto wallet apps.

### 2.5 Glass OS widgets
- **Look:** Desktop wallpaper with frosted widgets for calendar, notes and system stats.
- **Buttons:** Tiny glass icon buttons in widget corners for settings and expand.
- **Nav pill:** Glass dock-style pill at the bottom for switching widget pages.
- **Sidebar:** Frosted notification centre sliding in from the right.
- **Use case:** Desktop widgets, launchers and smart-display home screens.

### 3.1 Soft smart-home remote
- **Look:** Light grey surface with raised circular dials for temperature and lights.
- **Buttons:** Raised round buttons that look pressed in (inner shadow) when on; the active state adds a small coloured icon.
- **Nav pill:** Recessed track with a raised knob sliding between Rooms, Scenes and Energy.
- **Sidebar:** Raised room cards stacked vertically, with the selected room pressed in.
- **Use case:** Smart-home controllers and thermostat apps.

### 3.2 Neumorphic calculator
- **Look:** One soft beige surface; the display is a long recessed panel at the top.
- **Buttons:** Raised rounded-square keys that sink in on press; operators carry an orange label.
- **Nav pill:** Recessed toggle for Basic and Scientific.
- **Sidebar:** A history tape in a recessed column on wider screens.
- **Use case:** Calculator and unit-converter apps.

### 3.3 Fitness ring tracker
- **Look:** Off-white surface with a recessed circular track and a raised progress ring inside it.
- **Buttons:** Raised pill buttons for Start workout; secondary buttons are flatter, with a smaller shadow.
- **Nav pill:** Bottom bar pressed into the surface; the active icon sits on a raised dot.
- **Sidebar:** Weekly stats in raised cards with recessed mini bar charts.
- **Use case:** Fitness, step-count and habit trackers.

### 3.4 Soft audio mixer
- **Look:** Grey surface with recessed slider tracks and raised knobs.
- **Buttons:** Raised square mute and solo buttons that light their label when pressed.
- **Nav pill:** Recessed segmented control for Mix, EQ and Effects.
- **Sidebar:** A channel list as raised strips; the selected channel is pressed in.
- **Use case:** Audio apps, DJ tools and equaliser settings.

### 3.5 Neumorphic settings panel
- **Look:** Single-colour page with each setting group in a softly raised card.
- **Buttons:** Toggle switches with a recessed track and a raised knob; save is a raised pill.
- **Nav pill:** Recessed tabs for General, Privacy and Notifications.
- **Sidebar:** Raised icon list on the left, with the active item pressed in.
- **Use case:** Settings screens for device companion apps.

## 4. Liquid glass

Glass that bends and refracts what's behind it, with a moving highlight.
Traits: lens-like distortion, specular edges, light that responds to motion.

### 4.1 Refractive tab bar
- **Look:** Content scrolls under a floating bar made of thick glass that bends the content behind it.
- **Buttons:** Glass droplet buttons with a bright rim highlight that follows the device tilt or pointer.
- **Nav pill:** A liquid lens slides between tabs and stretches like a droplet while moving.
- **Sidebar:** A glass panel that magnifies the edge of the page behind it as it slides in.
- **Use case:** Mobile apps on modern OS designs, photo galleries and media apps.

### 4.2 Lens product viewer
- **Look:** A product photo with a draggable glass lens that magnifies and distorts as it moves.
- **Buttons:** Small glass bead buttons for colour options, each refracting its own colour.
- **Nav pill:** Glass pill for Front, Side and Back views; the selection is a moving lens.
- **Sidebar:** A specs panel in thick glass, with the product softly visible behind it.
- **Use case:** Premium product pages for phones, watches and jewellery.

### 4.3 Liquid control centre
- **Look:** Rounded glass tiles over the wallpaper, with highlights that shimmer when touched.
- **Buttons:** Circular glass toggles that ripple outward when switched.
- **Nav pill:** A glass slider for brightness and volume that bulges where your finger is.
- **Sidebar:** A glass shortcut tray at the edge that bends icons as it opens.
- **Use case:** OS quick settings and smart-home control panels.

### 4.4 Glass media controls
- **Look:** Full-bleed video with floating glass controls that refract the frame behind them.
- **Buttons:** Play, skip and volume as glass bubbles with a moving specular highlight.
- **Nav pill:** Scrubber bar as a glass tube; the playhead is a droplet.
- **Sidebar:** Chapter list in a refractive side panel that fades in on hover.
- **Use case:** Video players and streaming apps.

### 4.5 Liquid landing hero
- **Look:** Bold headline behind a large slow-moving glass blob that warps the text.
- **Buttons:** Primary CTA as a glass capsule over a solid colour; the hover makes the rim brighter.
- **Nav pill:** Floating glass nav pill at the top, with a lens that follows the hovered link.
- **Sidebar:** None on the landing page; a glass mobile menu drops down on small screens.
- **Use case:** Product launch pages and brand sites.

## 5. Bento grid

Content arranged in tidy tiles of different sizes, like a bento box.
Traits: modular grid, rounded cards, one idea per tile.

Takeaways: use one fixed grid and let tiles span cells so rows stay
aligned (unlike masonry); one idea per tile, with the most important idea
in the biggest tile; plan the phone order on purpose, because bento
grids don't stack well on their own.

### 5.1 Feature bento landing page
- **Look:** One large hero tile plus smaller tiles for features, each with an icon, short copy or a mini demo.
- **Buttons:** Small arrow buttons in tile corners; the main CTA sits in its own tile.
- **Nav pill:** Sticky rounded pill at the top with section links and a dark "Get started" end cap.
- **Sidebar:** None; the grid is the navigation.
- **Use case:** SaaS and product marketing pages.

### 5.2 Personal portfolio bento
- **Look:** Tiles for photo, bio, current project, music, location map and social links.
- **Buttons:** Social tiles are themselves buttons; the hover lifts the tile and shows an arrow.
- **Nav pill:** A small pill for Work, About and Contact that scrolls to tile groups.
- **Sidebar:** None on desktop; tiles reflow into one column on mobile.
- **Use case:** Personal sites and link-in-bio pages.

### 5.3 Bento analytics dashboard
- **Look:** Tiles of different sizes for KPIs, charts, recent activity and goals.
- **Buttons:** Ghost icon buttons for expand, filter and export on each tile.
- **Nav pill:** Date-range pill: Today, 7 days, 30 days, Custom.
- **Sidebar:** Slim left rail for switching between dashboards.
- **Use case:** Analytics, sales and marketing dashboards.

### 5.4 Bento app home
- **Look:** Home screen of tiles for quick actions, recent files, a calendar peek and a weather tile.
- **Buttons:** Whole tiles act as buttons, with a press-in scale effect.
- **Nav pill:** Bottom pill with Home, Search and Profile.
- **Sidebar:** On tablet, a column of pinned tiles on the left.
- **Use case:** Productivity app home screens and personal dashboards.

### 5.5 Bento product spec sheet
- **Look:** Tiles for battery, chip, camera, display and materials, each with one big number.
- **Buttons:** "Compare" and "Buy" in a sticky tile at the bottom.
- **Nav pill:** Pill to switch between product models; tile values animate on change.
- **Sidebar:** None; a compare drawer slides up from the bottom.
- **Use case:** Tech product pages and spec comparisons.

## 6. Minimalism

Only what's needed, with lots of space around it.
Traits: generous whitespace, limited palette, strong type hierarchy.

### 6.1 Minimal reading app
- **Look:** Off-white page, one serif typeface, wide margins, nothing on screen but the text.
- **Buttons:** Text-only buttons in small caps; icons only for back and settings.
- **Nav pill:** Hidden until scroll-up, then a thin pill with chapter title and progress.
- **Sidebar:** A table of contents that slides in as a plain list with no icons.
- **Use case:** E-readers, blogs and long-form article pages.

### 6.2 Minimal to-do list
- **Look:** Single column of tasks, lots of space, one accent colour for the checkbox.
- **Buttons:** One floating "+" button; everything else is a swipe or tap gesture.
- **Nav pill:** Small pill for Today, Upcoming and Done.
- **Sidebar:** A list of project names, text only, with a count on the right.
- **Use case:** Task managers and note apps.

### 6.3 Minimal portfolio
- **Look:** Large images with thin captions, a monochrome palette and one sans-serif face.
- **Buttons:** Underlined text links instead of buttons.
- **Nav pill:** Name on the left and three text links on the right, no background.
- **Sidebar:** None; a project index appears as a plain list page.
- **Use case:** Designer, photographer and architect portfolios.

### 6.4 Minimal checkout
- **Look:** One step per screen, a single input group centred, the total always visible.
- **Buttons:** One full-width black button per step; back is a text link.
- **Nav pill:** Thin step indicator: Details, Shipping, Payment.
- **Sidebar:** An order summary in a light grey column on desktop, collapsed on mobile.
- **Use case:** Checkout and payment flows.

### 6.5 Minimal settings
- **Look:** Grouped rows with labels on the left and controls on the right, divided by thin lines.
- **Buttons:** Plain toggles and chevrons; destructive actions in red text only.
- **Nav pill:** None; a simple back arrow and title.
- **Sidebar:** On desktop, a text list of setting groups with the active one in bold.
- **Use case:** App settings and account pages.

### 7.5 Maximal creator profile

- **Nav pill:** A ribbon of tabs styled as tickets or tags.
- **Sidebar:** A widget column with guestbook, now-playing and badges.
- **Use case:** Creator profiles and personal fan sites.

## 8. Skeuomorphism

Interfaces that imitate real objects and materials.
Traits: realistic textures, physical controls, depth and lighting.

### 8.1 Leather notebook journal
- **Look:** A stitched leather cover opens onto lined paper pages with a ribbon bookmark.
- **Buttons:** Embossed leather tabs and a metal clasp for locking entries.
- **Nav pill:** Coloured paper tabs sticking out of the page edge for each section.
- **Sidebar:** An index card drawer with entries on paper cards.
- **Use case:** Journal, diary and note-taking apps.

### 8.2 Analogue camera app
- **Look:** Brushed-metal body, a leatherette grip and a glass viewfinder.
- **Buttons:** A big chrome shutter button with a click; knurled dials for ISO and shutter speed.
- **Nav pill:** A mode dial (Photo, Video, Portrait) that rotates with detents.
- **Sidebar:** A film-roll strip of recent shots along the side.
- **Use case:** Camera apps and photo filter apps.

### 8.3 Hi-fi music player
- **Look:** A wooden cabinet, a spinning vinyl record and glowing VU meters.
- **Buttons:** Chrome toggle switches and rotary knobs for volume and tone.
- **Nav pill:** Push-button selectors labelled Phono, Tape and Tuner.
- **Sidebar:** A record crate you flip through to pick an album.
- **Use case:** Music players and vinyl collection apps.

### 8.4 Desk calendar planner
- **Look:** A tear-off paper calendar on a wooden desk with a pen and sticky notes.
- **Buttons:** Sticky notes as add buttons; a rubber stamp marks events as done.
- **Nav pill:** Metal ring binder tabs for Day, Week and Month.
- **Sidebar:** A corkboard with pinned reminders.
- **Use case:** Calendar and planning apps.

### 8.5 Retro game console menu
- **Look:** Plastic console shell with a CRT screen inset and cartridge slots.
- **Buttons:** Glossy plastic A/B buttons and a d-pad that depress on click.
- **Nav pill:** A cartridge selector that slides cartridges in and out.
- **Sidebar:** A shelf of game boxes with spine art.
- **Use case:** Game launchers and retro emulator front ends.

## 9. Material Design

Google's system of paper-like layers with consistent motion.
Traits: elevation shadows, bold colour, ripple feedback, grid spacing.

### 9.1 Material email client
- **Look:** White surfaces at set elevations, a coloured top app bar and a list of message cards.
- **Buttons:** Floating action button for Compose; text buttons with ripple feedback.
- **Nav pill:** Navigation bar at the bottom with an active indicator pill behind the icon.
- **Sidebar:** Navigation drawer with labelled folders and a tinted active row.
- **Use case:** Email, messaging and inbox apps.

### 9.2 Material task board
- **Look:** Columns of elevated cards with chips for labels and avatars for assignees.
- **Buttons:** Filled tonal buttons for Add card; icon buttons for menus.
- **Nav pill:** Segmented buttons for Board, List and Calendar.
- **Sidebar:** Navigation rail with icons and short labels for projects.
- **Use case:** Project management and kanban tools.

### 9.3 Material food delivery app
