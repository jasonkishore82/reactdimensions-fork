# 17 UI/UX styles

Visual styles to draw on for the library's UI category. Each entry gives a
short description and the traits that make the style recognisable.

1. **Flat UI**
   Solid colours, no shadows or textures, simple icons and clear type.
   Traits: flat fills, bold colour blocks, crisp edges.

2. **Glassmorphism**
   Frosted, semi-transparent panels floating over a colourful background.
   Traits: background blur, light translucent fill, thin bright border.

3. **Neumorphism**
   Soft shapes that look pressed into or raised out of the same surface.
   Traits: one base colour, paired light and dark shadows, low contrast.

4. **Liquid glass**
   Glass that bends and refracts what's behind it, with a moving highlight.
   Traits: lens-like distortion, specular edges, light that responds to motion.

5. **Bento grid**
   Content arranged in tidy tiles of different sizes, like a bento box.
   Traits: modular grid, rounded cards, one idea per tile.

6. **Minimalism**
   Only what's needed, with lots of space around it.
   Traits: generous whitespace, limited palette, strong type hierarchy.

7. **Maximalism**
   Loud, layered and full: more colour, pattern and type, on purpose.
   Traits: clashing colours, dense layouts, mixed fonts, big imagery.

8. **Skeuomorphism**
   Interfaces that imitate real objects and materials.
   Traits: realistic textures, physical controls, depth and lighting.

9. **Material Design**
   Google's system of paper-like layers with consistent motion.
   Traits: elevation shadows, bold colour, ripple feedback, grid spacing.

10. **Neo-brutalism**
    Raw and blunt, with heavy outlines and bright flat colour.
    Traits: thick black borders, hard offset shadows, plain type.

11. **Claymorphism**
    Puffy, soft 3D shapes that look like modelled clay.
    Traits: big rounded corners, inner and outer shadows, pastel colours.

12. **Aurora UI**
    Soft, blurred gradients that drift like the northern lights.
    Traits: blended colour blobs, heavy blur, slow movement.

13. **Isometric UI**
    Elements drawn on an isometric grid to fake 3D depth.
    Traits: 30-degree angles, stacked blocks, no vanishing point.

14. **Frutiger Aero**
    The glossy, optimistic look of the mid-2000s: sky, water and glass.
    Traits: glossy highlights, bubbles, nature imagery, aqua and green.

15. **3D UI**
    Real 3D objects and scenes as part of the interface.
    Traits: rendered models, lighting and materials, interactive camera.

16. **Y2K aesthetic**
    Turn-of-the-millennium futurism: chrome, neon and pixel type.
    Traits: chrome and holographic finishes, bright colours, techy fonts.

17. **Dark UI**
    Light content on dark surfaces, easy on the eyes at night.
    Traits: near-black backgrounds, raised lighter surfaces, muted accents.
